Summary for Users & Surveyors

FieldPlus is an enterprise field inspection and survey data collection application. We collect GPS coordinates, photos, videos, and signatures exclusively for survey submission accuracy, fieldwork proof-of-work, and synchronization with your organization's backend server. Your data is stored locally in an encrypted/private SQLite database until synchronized with your configured organization server.

01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FieldPlus ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data and information when you install and use the FieldPlus mobile application for Android devices.

By using FieldPlus, you consent to the data practices described in this document. If you or your organization do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the application.

02 Information We Collect

When you use FieldPlus to perform inspections, audits, and fieldwork, the App collects the following categories of data:

  • Precise and Coarse Location (GPS): Latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy radius, and waypoint timestamps when you capture survey coordinates or track a field journey.
  • Photographs and Video Recordings: Images and video clips captured through the camera or chosen from your device gallery to document site inspections, defects, or verification evidence.
  • Digital Signatures: Vector signature touchpaths recorded on screen to certify inspection completion or client authorization.
  • Survey and Audit Responses: Text input, numerical entries, dropdown choices, checklists, and timestamps associated with dynamic forms.
  • Surveyor / User Identification: Surveyor name, email address, or device identifier used to authenticate your session against your organization's server.
  • Technical & Device Information: Device model, Android OS version, app version (1.3.3), network connectivity state, and local sync queue statuses.

04 How We Use Your Data

The collected information is used solely for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To generate complete inspection and survey audit reports.
  • To provide GPS proof-of-presence and verify the physical location where the fieldwork took place.
  • To allow offline caching and outbox queueing, enabling field teams to work without cellular coverage and upload data later.
  • To display submission history, media evidence galleries, and audit trails within your mobile app and web administration dashboard.
  • To prevent fraud and maintain the integrity of organizational inspections.

05 Data Storage & Security

We prioritize the security of your field data:

  • Local Device Storage: All pending submissions and captured evidence are stored within the application's sandboxed private storage (`app-specific storage`) and private SQLite database (Room). Other third-party apps on the device cannot access these files.
  • Data Transmission: Form submissions, media attachments, and authentication tokens are transmitted to your organization's API server over secure HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections (or designated intranet endpoints).
  • No Public Sharing: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or publicly disclose your personal data, survey answers, or media attachments to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

06 Third-Party Services & APIs

Depending on your organization's deployment configuration, FieldPlus may interact with the following services:

  • Map Providers (Google Maps / OpenStreetMap): Used for displaying submission location maps, coordinates preview, and surveyor routing. Maps render location tiles according to the chosen provider's standard privacy policies.
  • Organization Backend API: FieldPlus communicates directly with the web admin server configured in your App Settings (e.g., your company's dedicated FieldPlus server instance).

07 Data Retention & User Rights

Survey data stored locally on your device can be cleared at any time via the Submissions > Options > Clear All Submissions feature or by clearing the app data in Android system settings. Once synchronized to your organization's backend database, data retention is managed by your organization's system administrator according to applicable enterprise retention policies and regulatory requirements.

08 Children's Privacy

FieldPlus is an enterprise tool built exclusively for professional surveyors, technicians, and organizational personnel. The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
